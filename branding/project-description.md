# BuddyRotation

Build a spell sequence straight from your spellbook in World of Warcraft Forever. Use one action-bar key to play the order you chose, one press at a time.

Open your spellbook and click **BuddyRotation**, or type **/buddyrotation** outside combat. Give your sequence a name, pick spells and arrange them in casting order. Repeat individual steps, add an opener that runs once per target, or choose when the sequence resets. Save it and drag the character macro onto your action bar.

Your sequences belong to the character that created them. The editor is available in English and German.

**Already using ForeverBuddy?** BuddyRotation is included there. The standalone download is for players who want just the spell-sequence editor.

[Watch the hunter test](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/2940034482)

## Deutsch

Mit BuddyRotation erstellst du eigene Zauberfolgen direkt im Zauberbuch von WoW Forever. Öffne **BuddyRotation** im Zauberbuch oder gib **/buddyrotation** ein. Wähle Zauber, Reihenfolge und Wiederholungen und ziehe das gespeicherte Charaktermakro auf deine Aktionsleiste. Jeder Tastendruck löst einen Schritt aus. Wer ForeverBuddy nutzt, hat den Editor bereits.
